My choices where §8.5 is silent: `NOT NULL` on every column except `sample`, `git_sha`, `completed_at`, `result_uri` and the task state fields; foreign keys `run.dataset_id → dataset`, `run.priors_hash → priors_spec`, `task.run_id → run` (`PRAGMA foreign_keys = ON`); `CHECK` constraints pin `run.status`, `task.status` and `priors_spec.status` to their enum values; times stored as ISO 8601 text with offset. "In flight" is `pending` or `running` (A5); `timeout`, `out_of_memory`, `preempted`, `cancelled`, `unknown` are not, to revisit with Slurm requeue in Phase 2. `completed_at` is stamped on entering `complete`.

- Finding on B3: on white noise this estimate is 0.864 σ, not σ, because a 9-point median residual is exactly 0 wherever a sample is its own window's median (1 in 9), which shrinks the MAD (measured 0.8589 ± 0.093 over 2000 draws of 171 channels; 0.8643 on 10⁶). `test_noise_sd_of_white_noise_near_sigma` asserts 0.864 σ ± 5 % and says why. - Despike: running median over `kernel` channels (`mode="nearest"`); a sample whose residual from it exceeds `thresh` normal-scaled MADs of the residual (either sign) is replaced by the running median. At very high signal-to-noise a 3-channel median can shave about 1 % off a narrow peak's top; `test_adjacent_real_peak_preserved` asserts the peak height within 2 % and its position within one channel.
